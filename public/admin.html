Publisher desk

Bring a new idea
into the world.

Paste one JSON post or a whole collection. It will be published immediately to the blog database.

Accepted format

{
  "main header": "How to get rich",
  "header1": "Wake up early",
  "body1": "Your first section...",
  "header2": "Protect your focus",
  "body2": "Your second section..."
}

You can also use title, body, excerpt, category, date, and image. A ready-to-edit example is included in sample-post.json.
